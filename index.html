E-waste is valuable, and easy to leave in a drawer.

No feedback

People rarely see what their recycling achieves, so the habit fades.

Hard to engage

Colleges and businesses want green results but lack a simple way to involve their people.

Inconsistent supply

Recycling partners need engaged communities that return sorted material reliably.

One station that makes recycling worth doing.

TESORO POINT links people who recycle with the organizations and recycling partners that need them. A smart collection station identifies and sorts each item, recognizes positive action, and shows the impact a community creates together.
